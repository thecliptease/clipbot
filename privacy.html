Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information ClipBot handles and what happens to it. ClipBot is a desktop app operated by The Clip Tease (“we”, “us”). It runs only on our own computer and is used only with our own accounts.

What ClipBot does

ClipBot watches public Twitch streams we choose, measures how busy each chat is, and saves short highlight clips when chat activity spikes. It adds captions and posts selected clips to The Clip Tease's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Page and TikTok accounts through each platform's official API.

Information ClipBot handles

Our own accounts

When we connect our YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Page, TikTok or Twitch account, the platform gives ClipBot an access token. ClipBot uses it only to:

  • read basic details of our account: its ID, name or username, the linked Facebook Page and Instagram account, follower and post counts, and which permissions we granted;
  • upload and publish our clips, and check their processing status and posting limits;
  • read the captions and dates of our recent posts, so the same clip is never posted twice.

Tokens are stored only on our computer, in a file that only our user account can read. ClipBot doesn't read messages, comments or contacts, and it never accesses anyone else's account.

Public Twitch streams and chat

ClipBot reads public stream details (whether a channel is live, its title and game) and records the public video, keeping only a rolling buffer of about 15 minutes that is continuously overwritten. Only highlight clips are saved.

It reads public chat through Twitch's official chat service, with read-only access, and never sends messages. For each chat message it stores the time, the text, and a salted one-way hash in place of the viewer's account ID. Usernames are not stored. Chat logs are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Highlight clips

Saved clips, their captions (transcribed on our computer, not by an online service), titles and descriptions stay on our computer until we delete them. Clips we post are public on our accounts and credit the streamer with a link to their channel.

Visitors to this website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to run its service; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. ClipBot sends information only to the platform it came from or to the platform a clip is posted to, over encrypted connections.

Google and YouTube

ClipBot uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. ClipBot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page.

Meta and TikTok

ClipBot uses the Meta Graph API (Facebook and Instagram) and the TikTok Content Posting API only as described above. Access can be removed at any time in Facebook's “Apps and websites” settings, Instagram's “Website permissions” settings, or TikTok's app permission settings.

Retention

  • Stream video buffer: continuously overwritten, about 15 minutes.
  • Chat logs: deleted automatically after 14 days.
  • Clips and captions: kept until we delete them.
  • Access tokens: kept until we disconnect the account in ClipBot, which deletes them.

To ask us to delete information about you, follow the Data Deletion Instructions.

Children

ClipBot isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
