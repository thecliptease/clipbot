ClipBot

The highlight tool behind The Clip Tease.

ClipBot is a desktop app that runs on one computer. It watches a few public Twitch streams, notices when a chat suddenly gets much busier than usual, and saves the 35 seconds around that moment as a vertical clip with captions.

The best clips are posted to The Clip Tease's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts, crediting the streamer with a link to their channel.

Who uses it

Only The Clip Tease. ClipBot isn't a public service: there's nothing to sign up for, and it never signs in to anyone else's accounts.

Streamers

If you'd like a clip taken down, or would rather your channel not be clipped, email ytdellmon@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.
