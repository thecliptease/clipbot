Data Deletion Instructions

Effective November 17, 2026

ClipBot keeps its data on The Clip Tease's own computer and signs in only to The Clip Tease's own accounts. It never receives information from your Facebook, Instagram, Google or TikTok account.

Ask us to delete your information

  1. Email ytdellmon@gmail.com with the subject “ClipBot data deletion request”.
  2. Include your Twitch username, or a link to the clip or post you mean, so we can find the data.
  3. Within 30 days we'll delete what we hold about you (chat log lines, and clips of your stream with their captions), remove any of those clips we posted, and reply to confirm.

Chat logs are also deleted automatically after 14 days.

Remove ClipBot's access to a Facebook or Instagram account

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → ClipBot → Remove.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → ClipBot → Remove.

Once access is removed, any token ClipBot stored for that account stops working, and we delete it.
